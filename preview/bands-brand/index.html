Rent vs Buy

Home decision calculator

Renting and buying both hide their real costs. Rent vs Buy compares them on total net worth — not the monthly payment — so you can see which one actually leaves you better off, and when.

  • Opportunity cost of your down payment, invested
  • Equity, appreciation, taxes and upkeep modelled
  • The exact year buying overtakes renting

Doomsday

The day-of-the-week party trick

Name the day of the week for any date in history, in your head, in a few seconds. Doomsday teaches the algorithm one step at a time and drills you until the arithmetic disappears and it just feels like knowing.

  • Learn the method in guided steps

  • Works for any date, past or future
